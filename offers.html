Privilege FAQs

Promotional privileges must be applied during initial checkout. However, Royal Gold Connoisseurs can contact the concierge butler desk up to 2 hours prior to showtime to apply eligible concessions upgrades.

Only one ticket discount code may be applied per transaction. However, food and beverage privileges (such as SNACKCOMBO) can be added alongside ticket codes at the concession desk.

These privileges are demonstrative frontend features designed for THEATRON Member 4 testing. Clicking 'Copy Code' copies the exact string to your system clipboard with real-time UI feedback.

Royal Gold members enjoy complimentary cancellation and reschedule privileges up to 60 minutes prior to feature presentation commencement.
