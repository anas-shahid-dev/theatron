About THEATRON

Conceived in the heart of F-7 Markaz, Islamabad, THEATRON Cinema Lounges reimagines film exhibition as private architectural hospitality. Every surface—from sound-absorbing obsidian walnut paneling to hand-tufted crimson velvet—is tuned to eliminate distraction and heighten emotional resonance.

Instead of crowded multiplex rows, our guests settle into spacious Italian motorized recliners with discreet tableside service, dual-laser 4K projection, and bespoke acoustic chambers calibrated to mastering-studio standards.
